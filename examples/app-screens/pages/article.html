Why we rewrote our layout engine

Three years of patches taught us that layout is a contract, not a heuristic.

When we started, our renderer measured text twice and hoped for the best. That worked until right-to-left support landed and every edge case came due at once.

What changed

The new engine runs in three passes. Each one has a single job, and each one is tested against the browser with layout:vectors.

  • Intrinsic sizes are computed once and cached.
  • Line breaking follows UAX #14 exactly.
  • Every box is compared against Chrome to the sub-pixel.

Make it work, make it right, make it fast, in that order.

function layout(node) {
  const size = measure(node);
  return place(node, size);
}

The result is 40% faster on our slowest screen, and far easier to reason about.
