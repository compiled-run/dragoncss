Dragon lays out its pages with an engine of its own making, written in TypeScript: block, flex, text, positioning, and script that runs right to left.

And it is proven, not by screenshots but number by number against Chrome. Every box is weighed†, every computed value compared‡, and the trials are counted§.

Where a thing cannot be done, the scribe does not keep silent. What the compiler cannot decide becomes a build error that carries its own fix, never a surprise upon a device. Each property bears its mark‖.

The web is proven this day. The iPhone and the Android are being copied fair in the second campaign, each checked against Chrome in its turn.

Dragon CSS, plainly

  • Regular CSS, compiled at build time into native view properties. One CSS for web, iOS and Android.
  • Its own TypeScript layout engine: block, flex, text, positioning, right-to-left.
  • Proven against Chrome by numbers, not screenshots: 137 fixtures, 258 cases, 8,671 of 8,671 boxes exact to 1/64 px, 339,080 computed values identical.
  • Support labels: exact, caveat, unsupported. Nothing is marked supported without a passing test. What the compiler cannot decide is a build error with a fix.
