Dear Lord Developer,

A dragon keeps the gate between thy stylesheet and the devices of the realm. He is no tyrant. What is proven, he lets pass; what is not, he stops at build time, before it reaches any phone.

He never guesses. In the first campaign he held 8,671 boxes against Chrome and found every one exact, to 1/64 of a pixel.

When he refuses a line, he tells thee why and hands thee the fix. Offer him a line of CSS below, and hear his verdict.

    The plain summary

    • Dragon compiles regular CSS at build time into native view properties, proven against Chrome number by number, never by screenshots.
    • It has its own TypeScript layout engine: block, flex, text, positioning and right-to-left.
    • Milestone 1: 137 test fixtures and 258 cases pass. 8,671 of 8,671 boxes match Chrome exactly, to 1/64 px. 339,080 computed values are identical between the author's CSS and Dragon's output.
    • Support is labelled exact, caveat or unsupported. Nothing is marked supported without a passing test. Anything else is refused at build time with a diagnostic: a code, a message, why, and a fix.
    • Now in progress (milestone 2): iOS and Android at parity. Native engines are generated from the one TypeScript engine, and each device lane is checked against Chrome.
