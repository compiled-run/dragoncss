Dear Lord Developer,

Word has reached the Round Table that you still write your styles twice: once for the web, and once more for every device in the realm.

Lay down that burden. Dragon takes regular CSS and, at build time, compiles it into native view properties.

We ask no faith of you. Dragon keeps its own layout engine, and it is proven against Chrome number by number, never by screenshots: 8,671 of 8,671 boxes match to 1/64 of a pixel.

Every property bears an honest mark, exact, caveat or unsupported, and none is called supported without a passing test.

Dragon CSS, plainly

  • Regular CSS, compiled at build time into native view properties.
  • Its own TypeScript layout engine: block, flex, text, positioning, right-to-left.
  • Proven against Chrome by numbers, not screenshots: 137 fixtures, 258 cases, 8,671 of 8,671 boxes exact to 1/64 px, 339,080 computed values identical.
  • Support labels: exact, caveat, unsupported. Nothing is marked supported without a passing test.
  • Road ahead: web proven (milestone 1). iOS and Android at parity, in progress (milestone 2). Custom properties (for utility CSS such as Tailwind) later, then 1.0.
