TXT1a-2 is one landing, in three checkpoint phases.

**Phase C, compiler.**
- project.ts checkCases computes engineFacesOf once per case and passes realFaceAt(address) to checkFonts and checkComputed. realFaceAt is true when a real non-Ahem face resolves.
- checkFonts raises nothing for such a node; otherwise it raises the existing refusal and deferral.
- The native lowerTree call gets faceOf from the same map. ios-layout lowerFamily in native mode uses faceOf, and Ahem stays AHEM_FACE.
- computed-checks checkUserAgentDefaults skips the ua-font refusal only where realFaceAt holds, per target. That is the real Lato-Bold-under-h1–h3 blocker.
- textFontProblem and isFontProfileRefusal stay unchanged.

**Phase F, fixtures, measurers and lanes.**
- The TEXT_LATIN_FIXTURES export is removed. TEXT_LATIN and TEXT_LATIN_FACES are appended to FIXTURES, along with the Ahem fractional-size fixtures (7.77, 9.99, 11.1111, 17.3, 23.3, 33.33 px) and text-calibration.
- fonts.ts MAPS gains the entries.
- pipeline.ts runFixture runs the per-node platformFonts face check for every listed id in each direction at DPR 1, and applies joinHyphenRects. No registry check is lost.
- The registry loops are removed from the capture, dpr-capture, report and gen-profile-rows CLIs. Captures move to expected/** and expected-dpr/**, deep-equal to the old expected-text-latin, which is then deleted.
- A case is shaped when its compiled input names a non-Ahem face. This is derived; there is no flag. Shaped vectors stay in vectors/text-latin/**.
- corpus-dpr.ts appends one text-latin suite; existing suite counts are unchanged.
- The measurers (native-host referenceMeasurer, lanes referenceProof, break-vectors, line-breaks) use shapedMeasurerFor for every face. The Swift and Kotlin line-breaks harnesses replay transcripts.

**Phase R, runtime and devices.** It starts only once p6a-promote, seld-lanes and seld-r1a are in BASE, because of the spec's P6 non-concurrency rule.
- The Swift and Kotlin GlyphShaper runs over the T081 bridges.
- Face sha256 checks (R8).
- Glyph ids are drawn at engine positions.
- The shim is linked and the faces bundled.
- The R10 rule, with its threshold written into the test.
- Six runtime plants; shaperFromPlatform is caught by device-frames.
- replay-device.ts gets an additive --transcript flag.
- The device step also waits for the INL1a native:encoders fix.

**Android.** No Android-only code; regenerate when #49 reaches the stack.

**Pins.**
