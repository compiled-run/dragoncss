## Merge-train ruling (Judge T-LANDING-TRAIN, PM accepted 2026-10-01)

Option B is approved. Each member keeps its own Macroscope-clean PR, and the train builds its positions on one fixed origin/master.

**Positions.** Position k = branch k + position k-1 + one commit holding only `pnpm regen` output. Each position gets its own typecheck, test and device run.

**Landing a position.**
- Push position k only after PR k-1 has merged.
