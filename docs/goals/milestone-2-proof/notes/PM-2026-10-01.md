## Merge-train ruling (Judge T-LANDING-TRAIN, PM accepted 2026-10-01)

Option B is approved. Each member keeps its own Macroscope-clean PR, and the train builds its positions on one fixed origin/master.

**Positions.** Position k = branch k + position k-1 + one commit holding only `pnpm regen` output. Each position gets its own typecheck, test and device run.

**Landing a position.**
- Push position k only after PR k-1 has merged.
- Merge with `--match-head-commit`.
- After each merge, check that master's tree equals the position's tree, with docs/goals/** excepted.

**Evidence.** Every master merge commit carries device evidence from its own tree. A proof on the train tip alone is rejected, because lanes.ts:682 would then fail every earlier master commit.

**Rejected options.**
- One combined train PR: it would get a full paid review again.
- GitHub merge queue: it needs branch protection, which is an owner action, and Macroscope does not review its branches.
- Finer device evidence: deferred, because almost every queued member changes the engine.

**Order.** One train at a time, of at most 5 members. Train 1: SELD-R1a, PORT-0, EMS-a, EMS-b, p6a-dash-v2.

**Scripts.** scripts/merge-train.ts and merge-train-lib.ts, plus regenOnlyProblems in pr-review-vouch.ts. The worker is LAND-2.

**Rule changes.** The AGENTS.md step 7 and goal.md landing-queue wording change when the script lands.
