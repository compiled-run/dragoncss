**PM ruling on txt2a's letter-spacing rows.** txt2a marks `letter-spacing:<length-px>` exact in six contexts, but its only proof cases use the value 0, and the "only 0" limit is hard-coded in computed-checks `checkLetterSpacing`. "No claim without a test" outranks keeping the row. At the INL2 restack, narrow the rows' valueSubset to `0`, so the check matches what the profile proves. Part b then widens the rows with real proofs and removes the check.

**Shared-file constraint:** TXT2 part f and TDEC-b both edit native-support.ts, so they must never be in flight together.

**North star:** part a (txt2a) removes 5 errors; the later parts change nothing.
