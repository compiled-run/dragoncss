## Design choices and evidence (PM rule 2: decided by research, recorded here)

1. **Chrome break semantics.**
   - A code unit belongs to the line of its first Range client rect. Lines can overlap vertically, so the unit goes to the line box whose centre is nearest.
   - A soft-wrap space has a zero-width rect at the end of its line and another at the start of the next. The first counts, as in the engine.
   - A white-space unit with a zero-width first rect never starts a line. U+200B is not white space, so it can start one.
   - DOM offsets map to engine offsets through the phase-I collapse: a white-space run maps to its first unit.
   - Measured on text-wrap-spaces, collapsed-whitespace, projected-text and whitespace-leaves cases; the result is 966/966 equal.
2. **The glyph rule.**
   - Glyph boxes come from Ahem's own glyf bounding boxes (read from vendor/fonts/Ahem.ttf), scaled by size/unitsPerEm.
   - The pen follows the device exactly: float32 accumulation of size × advance / upem, starting from the line's absolute x. The baseline is the snapped line top plus the ascent.
   - Interior points are `glyph:<line>:<k>`. The edge scanlines across the first glyph's left edge and the last glyph's right edge use the `edge` kind (`edge:<line>:glyph-left|right`), so check (c) compares them by position within 1 device px.
   - Non-Ahem families are refused.
3. **Named checks for the real-dump faults:**
   - edge+2 and missing-node are checked by (a);
   - edge+1 and snap-disabled by (d);
   - applied-changed and applied-missing by (b);
   - channel-delta-1 by (c), on a sample that passed in the clean dump;
   - break-shifted by the break check.
   - "Caught" means the planted dump produces a problem the clean dump does not have.
4. **Stage placement.**
   - iOS: the stage sits at the safe-area origin (iPhone 17 at 0,186 px; iPad at 0,64).
   - Android: the stage is padded by the system-bar insets, because targetSdk 36 enforces edge-to-edge. It sits at 0,136 px.
   - Frames are read relative to the root, so this does not affect them. It lets the OS screenshot see the root unobstructed.
   - Below API 35 the decor consumes the insets, so the app also starts once the stage is laid out (this was the API 31 fix).
5. **Android capture settle.** The app captures only after the root is laid out and drawn and two more frames have passed. It then copies until two consecutive PixelCopy results are equal, retrying on ERROR_SOURCE_NO_DATA and ERROR_TIMEOUT.
   - Why: one run had captured a blank frame for position-absolute-percent-rtl@3. Freshly booted images under load also raised a System UI ANR dialog.
   - The runner therefore sets `hide_error_dialogs=1`, `svc power stayon true` and wake/dismiss-keyguard.
   - Capture-trust OS screenshots are taken once two consecutive screenshots are equal. An iPad screenshot had once caught the launch transition.
6. **ART vectors.** Under app_process, ART's heap growth limit (192 MB) cannot hold the 123 MB engine corpus that the harness reads whole. Each suite therefore runs in chunks of whole lines, at most 16 MB each; every line is an independent case, so the outputs concatenate. The harness is unchanged. A launcher runs its main on a thread with a 512 MB stack, as `-Xss` does on the JVM.
7. **Lanes file dragon.lanes/2.**
   - Each device lane records, per DPR set: the device record (name, model, OS/build, the scale from the profile and from the app, window/stage px, root offset, text scale), the dumps, the counts compared (a, b, c, d, breaks), the dumps' sha256 with timing removed, and failures by kind. It also keeps the first 20 failures.
   - device-pixels also carries the trust rows. Each target carries its dumpFaults rows.
   - checkLaneParity extends to run records through `runRecordProblems`.
   - `--run-host` or `--run-device` alone keeps the committed records of lanes it did not run, if they are not stale.
