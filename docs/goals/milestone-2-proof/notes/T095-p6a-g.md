## Findings for the PM (each needs a ruling; none is loosened here)

1. **Chrome's vertical fringe is one-sided, so the y-centre calibration fails.**
   - Core Graphics dilates the top edge and the left/right edges, not the bottom. At a 60 px glyph with integer edges, the row above the top edge reads 112 and the row below the bottom reads 255; left and right both read 187.
   - Chrome's y centre therefore sits 0.10 to 0.32 device px above the geometry, growing with size and saturating near 0.32 from 48 px up. The value is the same at every DPR and phase.
   - This exceeds the ruling's 0.25 bound: 336 of 504 y centres fail. The x axis is fine: at most 0.032, and fringe extent at most 0.41.
   - Consequence: clean-run y error is about +0.32 plus native noise, still under the 0.5 gate. An upward 1 px shift is caught with only about 0.18 margin, below 0.2. The downward y plant keeps about 0.8 margin.
   - The failing test is committed as is.
2. **The two position-absolute-out-of-flow@3 `edge:i2:bottom` entries do not remain.**
   - The i2 bottom edge (y = 105) coincides with the top edge of the b1c "Y" glyph (x 30 to 90), so every along-position of that scanline has clearance 0. Rule 1 drops it, and the dropped `border:i2:bottom` point has clearance 1.
   - The failing pixel (69,102) is itself 2 px clear. The Judge's count looked at that pixel only.
   - Keeping the entries needs either a ruling that allows a scanline with unclear pixels, or pixel-level clearance inside the check.
3. **iOS floors glyph x to whole device px, so iOS B3 will not reach 0 as counted.**
   - Evidence from units-em@3: pen 130.078 draws at 130.0, and 373.828 draws at 373.0, so the right edge is 0.83 px short. text-fractional-font-size@3 shows the same.
   - The cause is the default Core Graphics subpixel positioning and quantisation in `DragonTextView.draw`. The fix belongs in native-support.ts beyond the plant lines, so it is outside this package.
   - Expected on device: the 7 iOS glyph-ink-edge failures stay (units-em, units-absolute, text-fractional-font-size @3), plus about 6 or more iOS x-centre failures. Android should be clean.
   - Also 16 iOS and 24 Android B2 entries (selectors-universal-root) are dropped by the clearance; PNT1 loses those points.

## Deviations and interpretations

- **Interior candidates.** The interior rule has no single side, so it tries the 5×5 grid of along-positions, rows first, starting from the middle.
- **Middle-only fallback.** When the middle candidate does not fit geometry or the image, nothing is emitted and nothing counts as dropped, as before the ruling.
- **Glyph-top/bottom placement.** These are added per line on the first glyph with clear vertical scanlines. If none has them, both are dropped and counted.
- **Colour fallback.** A glyph-edge scanline without Chrome contrast is colour-checked at its two clear ends only (ruling 1 applied to the colour fallback).
- **Unchanged log line.** native-build.ts is outside the allowed files, so its plant log line still says "1 device px right" for the y plant.
- **Clearance of glyph interiors.** Clearance counts deep inside a glyph as clear, because the ink there is solid.

## iOS ink clipping fix (PM ruling), host side done; commit 893c87ca

**Choice: a drawing sublayer.** This is option 1 in the ruling.

- **Why not the other option.** A UIView's `draw(_:)` backing store holds only its bounds, whatever `clipsToBounds` or `masksToBounds` is set to. Growing the view would change the frame reported by the dump.
- **The layer.** `DragonTextView` now owns a `DragonGlyphLayer` (a CALayer subclass) whose frame is the view's bounds grown to every line's ink:
  - the ink is `CTFontGetBoundingBox` placed at each line's first and last glyph origin, including the plant offsets, which are the real drawing positions;
  - 2 device px are added for antialiasing;
  - the edges are floored or ceiled to whole device px, so glyph origins keep their fractional phase.
- **Drawing.** The layer's `draw(in:)` translates its context by its own origin and runs the same drawing code: same flags, positions and `CTFontDrawGlyphs`. `contentsScale` is set to the device scale, and the layer's implicit actions are off.
- **What is unchanged.** The view's frame, the dump's frames, glyph positions and coverage. The view no longer overrides `draw(_:)`.
- **Kotlin.** Byte-identical: the support sha256 for clean and both plants matches 18094685.
- **Host checks.** `pnpm typecheck` passes, `pnpm test` passes 1934/1934, and `native:build --target ios` passes clean and with both plants.
- **Still needed on device.** Both iOS plants caught with margin ≥ 0.2, iOS glyph-ink-edge 0, clean bottom errors recorded, and device-frames unchanged.
